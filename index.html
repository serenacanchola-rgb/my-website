Personalized high-volume support

AI helps me efficiently handle conversations that require a little more nurturing, allowing me to dedicate more time and attention to customers who are actively shopping and need more personalized support.

  1. The ask. A long, persuasive request for something I can’t change.
  2. The reply. Warm and honest about what I can’t do, and clear about what happens next: the feedback goes to the team.
  3. Why it matters. Conversations like this get a thoughtful answer quickly, and my attention stays on shoppers ready to buy.
